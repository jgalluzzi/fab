# FAB — Privacy Policy

FAB is a browser extension that hides Amazon Basics and other Amazon
private-label products from search results and listings on amazon.com.

## What FAB does with your data

FAB does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. Specifically:

- **No network requests.** FAB runs entirely inside your browser. It does
  not send any data to any server, including its own developer.
- **No tracking or analytics.**
- **Local settings only.** Your on/off preference and custom keyword list
  are stored using Chrome's built-in `storage` API. This data stays in
  your browser (synced across your own signed-in Chrome instances via
  Google's sync, if enabled) and is never accessible to us.
- **Page content.** FAB reads the text of product listings on amazon.com
  pages you visit, purely to decide whether to hide them. This happens
  locally in your browser and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

## Permissions

- `storage` — save your on/off setting and keyword list locally.
- Host access to `https://www.amazon.com/*` — required so the extension
  can run on Amazon pages and read listing text to filter them.
